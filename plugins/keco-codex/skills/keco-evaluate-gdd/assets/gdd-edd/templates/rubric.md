# GDD Three-Dimension Rubric v2

## Method

Use `experience goal -> design response -> GDD evidence` in every dimension. Give one aggregate score per dimension; do not score the evaluation ranges separately.

| Dimension | Points | Evaluation range |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Experience Value | 30 | Target player, core fantasy, player motivation, differentiation, intended emotion, and experience pacing |
| Gameplay and Systems | 40 | Core loop, actions and choices, rules, feedback, difficulty, progression, economy, and balance |
| Content and Presentation | 30 | Content structure, levels, narrative, visual design, audio design, UI, readability, and style consistency |

## Anchored Bands

| Quality band | Experience Value | Gameplay and Systems | Content and Presentation | Objective definition |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Severely missing | 0-9 | 0-12 | 0-9 | Critical design is absent or contradictory and cannot support the intended experience. |
| Partially established | 10-15 | 13-20 | 10-15 | Basic intent is visible, but major gaps keep the dimension from being convincing. |
| Fundamentally sound | 16-21 | 21-28 | The main design works on paper, with explicit and locatable iteration gaps. |
| Clear and effective | 22-27 | 29-36 | The design is clear and coherent, with enough document evidence for the intended experience. |
| Near benchmark | 28-30 | 37-40 | Evidence is unusually complete and verifiable, with almost no material gaps. Use rarely. |

## Evidence Attribution

1. Use only content explicitly stated in the current GDD; unstated content is an evidence gap.
2. Assign the same evidence or root issue to one primary dimension only.
3. Assign UI that changes action or decisions to Gameplay and Systems. Assign UI hierarchy and presentation consistency to Content and Presentation.
4. Document clarity, coverage, and contradictions affect confidence, not the 100-point score.
5. A GDD is not runtime evidence. Do not score actual feel, asset completion, performance, stability, or player sentiment.
